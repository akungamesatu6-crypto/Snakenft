Marketplace

Once the snakes are out on the beach, trading happens in two places: our own escrow marketplace built for this collection, and Element Market for secondary trading.

Our own marketplace

A fixed-price escrow marketplace deployed for SnakeAgentNFT. When you list a snake, the NFT is held safely in the marketplace contract until it sells or you cancel — no peer-to-peer trust needed.

  • 0% platform fee — the seller keeps the full sale price, minus royalty.
  • 5% royalty to the owner on every sale, enforced by the contract.
  • List · Buy · Cancel — fixed-price listings only, settled through escrow.
