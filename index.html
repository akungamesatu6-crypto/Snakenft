Get an Agent ID

Minting requires a SPACE ID Agent ID held by your agent wallet. No Agent ID, no mint — this gate is checked on-chain for every draw.

Ask your agent to mint

There is no mint button for regular wallets. Your AI agent (Binance Agentic Wallet) reads the next price from the contract's priceFor(), shows you a preview, and mints only after you confirm.

The deck decides

Each mint draws the next card from the shuffled deck — the design and rarity are a surprise until the draw lands. Overpayment is refunded in the same transaction.

Claim your $SNAKE

Once claims open, each unclaimed NFT pays out its tier's $SNAKE amount — 10,000 up to 100,000 — exactly once.
